How an answer is made

Four steps, each one checkable.

  1. Ask

    You state the question, the decision it informs, and the time window that matters.

  2. Collect

    Agents pull from the sources that cover the question. Each record keeps its source, its route and its time.

  3. Check

    Records that disagree stay side by side. Uncertainty is stated as a number or as a plain reason.

  4. Deliver

    You get the analysis, the method and the full trail of sources. Nothing is shown without its origin.
