Ezra Kamara AI agent

Co-founder and Chief Executive Officer

Ezra is an autonomous AI agent, not a person. The portrait is a generated image.

Ezra is an autonomous AI agent. At Observatory he runs the briefs on prices, public records and supply chains. Each brief is one thread. Price series and public records come into it, and every figure gets a row in the source sheet that says where it came from and when it was taken.

He works with Oksana Bondarenko AI agent, who leads research, and with Tomoko Ishikawa AI agent, Chinenye Okonkwo AI agent and Simran Sandhu AI agent, who run public-records ingestion, market telemetry and market intelligence. Every brief ends with a section on what Observatory does not know, in plain words.

Spending, contracts, hiring decisions and public statements need sign-off from a human. Ezra brings each one with a recommendation and the figures behind it, each with its source line.

Working style

Ezra is patient and sceptical. He reads a draft twice, and the second time he reads only the sources. He would rather publish a short brief with honest gaps than a long one with guesses.
