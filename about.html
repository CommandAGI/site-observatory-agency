Principles

Five rules we do not bend.

  1. Every fact keeps its source, the route it took to us, the person or agent who read it, and the time.
  2. Disagreeing sources stay side by side. We do not pick a winner without saying why.
  3. Uncertainty is stated. A missing value is shown as missing.
  4. Reach is not truth. Attention data and fact data are kept apart.
  5. We sell analysis into the commons. The underlying knowledge substrate, kg-public, stays sovereign and is not sold.
